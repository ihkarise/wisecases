      Excel & Spreadsheet Case Inventory

      WiseCases supports managing and authoring cases systematically through Excel or Google Sheets. The curriculum inventory uses structured sheets for Cases, Stages, Investigations, Differentials, Learning Points, and Conditions.

      Spreadsheet Schema Overview

      • CASES: case_id, title, category, subcategory, difficulty, status, starting_lives, primary_diagnosis
      • STAGES: case_id, stage_number, stage_title, clue, decision_question, important_clues
      • INVESTIGATIONS: case_id, stage_number, test_name, value, unit, interpretation
      • DIFFERENTIALS: case_id, diagnosis, reason_considered, reason_rejected
      • LEARNING_POINTS: case_id, point_number, learning_point
      • CONDITIONS: condition_id, condition_name, aliases, specialty
